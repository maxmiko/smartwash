Process

5 steps to a shine

A simple sequence on the EHRLE panel — adjust the programs to your liking.

  1. The active foam works across the whole surface at once and deeply loosens dirt, so the rest of the wash is more effective and gentler on the paint.

  2. MicroPowder is based on a special active-molecule formula and removes even the most stubborn dirt without brushes. Important: don't let the product dry on the car.

  3. Special salt tablets keep the water-softening unit effective and provide properly softened water — essential for a high-quality wash and prevents streaks.

  4. Together with warm water, the wax forms a protective film on the body: it repels water, shields the paint from dirt and adds shine — the car stays clean longer.

  5. After rinsing with demineralised water, a modern dryer quickly dries the car. You don't have to wipe the surface — it stays free of streaks and runs, clean and protected.
